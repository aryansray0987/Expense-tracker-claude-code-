Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Spendly personal expense tracking application and related services (the "Service"). We are committed to handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, together with the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information needed to operate the Service, such as log data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain the Service: to store and display your expenses, generate summaries of your spending, keep you signed in, and secure your account. We do not use your financial records for any purpose other than delivering the Service to you.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored so that it remains available to you across sessions. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law, or where strictly necessary to operate the Service (for example, infrastructure providers acting on our behalf).

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can access and update your account information at any time. You may delete your expense records or your entire account, after which the associated data is removed from the Service. If you have questions about your data, you can reach us through your account settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after any changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through your account settings.
